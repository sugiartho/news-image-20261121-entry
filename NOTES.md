   horizonapp.link (21 Nov).

   Daraxonrasib (Rasonque), a daily pill with chemotherapy: tumours

   EORTC-NCI-AACR symposium in Barcelona and led 21 Nov coverage.

   Medindia (21 Nov).

   news.kilambi.com (21 Nov).
7. **BepiColombo reaches Mercury.**
   The ESA/JAXA twin orbiters entered Mercury orbit on 21 Nov (18:24

   orbit Mercury. Sources: ESA factsheet and arrival updates, Sky &
   Telescope (21 Nov), earthsky.org (21 Nov).
